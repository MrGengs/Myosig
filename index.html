Background

Why Is MyoSig Needed?

Stroke is the leading cause of long-term disability worldwide, with prevalence increasing every year.

Stroke Problem

According to WHO, stroke is the 2nd leading cause of death and the primary cause of disability in adults. In Indonesia, stroke prevalence reaches 10.9 per 1,000 population.

Therapy Limitations

33% of stroke patients do not receive continuous motor training. Most clinical assessments cannot capture neuromuscular changes in real-time.

MyoSig Solution

A digital rehabilitation device with EMG + IMU sensors for objective monitoring and real-time neuromuscular recovery tracking, overcoming the limitations of subjective therapy.

Features

MyoSig Key Features

Combining advanced sensor technology with artificial intelligence for more effective rehabilitation.

Patient Data Management

Store patient profiles, track muscle assessment results over time, and review historical data to monitor recovery progress.

Muscle Activity Measurement (EMG)

Capture and analyze electrical muscle signals to assess patient muscle activation levels during rehabilitation.

Motion Measurement (IMU)

Track and analyze body movements using inertial sensors to assess movement patterns, range of motion, and motor performance.

Rehabilitation History Tracking

Record and monitor patient rehabilitation progress over time to evaluate improvements in muscle activity and movement.

AI-Based Recommendations

Provide intelligent recommendations that help clinicians design more personalized and effective rehabilitation programs for stroke patients.
